How radar listens

  • It brings its own signalSentinel-1 sends microwave pulses and listens for the echo, so darkness does not matter and cloud mostly does not block it.
  • Crops echo differentlyLeaves, stems and soil send back different amounts, in two channels called VV and VH.
  • It comes back every 12 daysOver this area there is one pass every 12 days. Stacked together they draw a winter-long signature for each field.

    Where it falls short

    • Labels come after harvestThe survey was done in May 2022, so each field's crop is recorded afterwards. It is not a random sample, so mapped areas are not area estimates.
    • Small fields blurA 10 m radar pixel often mixes neighbouring fields, and accuracy rises with field size.
    • Mustard and lentil are mergedThere are too few lentil fields to learn the two apart.
    • Some crops are missedThe class table above shows which crops the model finds and which it misses.

    What comes next

    • Add optical imagerySentinel-2 raised field macro-F1 from 0.560 to 0.609 in tests, but the ranges overlap, so it needs more fields to confirm.
    • Try a temporal neural networkA model that reads the whole season as a sequence is planned and not yet run.
    • Use L-band radarNISAR L-band data became public for passes from 17 June 2026, so a future season could use it. It cannot cover 2021-22.
